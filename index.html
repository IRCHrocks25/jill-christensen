About Jill

From laid-off executive to the world’s go-to voice on workplace culture.

In 2009, after 22 years climbing the ranks of corporate America — leading Global Internal Communications inside Fortune 500 companies — Jill Christensen was laid off. In that moment, she saw the truth she’d been living: too many organizations treat people like numbers, and it costs them everything. So she built a company to fix it.

Today, Jill is recognized as one of the highest-rated female speakers in the world. A Top 200 Global Thought Leader to Watch and Six Sigma Green Belt, she has shared her methodology with more than 500,000 professionals across 375 events worldwide. Her debut book is a global best-seller, and her award-winning blog was named a Top 100 Corporate Blog alongside Apple and Microsoft.

Off the stage: a dual U.S./U.K. citizen based in Arizona, you’ll find her at an NFL game, a rock concert, a pickleball court, or a Mahjong table.
